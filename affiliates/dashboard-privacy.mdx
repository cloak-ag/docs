description: "Why affiliates see grouped, delayed, rounded numbers, and never an individual person's transactions"

Your dashboard never shows what any one person did. It shows daily estimates that cover at least three different people, one day late, rounded down.

**The reason, in one sentence:** if your dashboard showed a day on which only one person used your link, you would know exactly how much that person moved and when, and Cloak exists so that nobody learns that.

The people you refer are Cloak users like any other, and their privacy does not depend on who sent them. The rules on this page are what make that true.

## What the dashboard shows

| You see | You never see |
| --- | --- |
| Your estimated commission for the current month, in USDC | Individual transactions |
| Daily estimates, one day late, only for days that cover at least three people | Wallet addresses of the people you referred |
| Daily clicks and conversions, one day late | Transaction signatures, amounts per person, or times finer than a day |
| Your monthly statements with exact amounts | How many transactions were made |
| Your commission rate, a fixed 20% | Anything that isolates a single person |

## The three rules

### 1. At least three people

A day's estimate appears only when it covers at least **three different people** who used your link. If a day covers fewer, it is not shown on its own: it is merged with the following days of the same month until the group reaches three people, and the combined estimate is shown on the last day of the group. A day that never reaches the floor within its month is left out of the daily view; its commission is not lost (see [the release floor](#the-release-floor)).

### 2. One day late

A day becomes visible at 00:00 UTC the following day, never while it is still in progress. Clicks and conversions follow the same delay. They are not subject to the three-person floor, because a count of clicks reveals no amounts.

### 3. Rounded down

Daily estimates are rounded down to steps of 0.10 USDC and valued at the current market price, so they are labeled **estimated**. Exact amounts appear only on closed monthly statements, which use the month's recorded [reference price](/affiliates/payouts#valuation-in-usdc).

## The release floor

The same three-person rule applies when a month closes. A month becomes a statement only if the commission it contains came from at least three different people. If it did not, the month's commission **carries over**: it is kept, and released together with a later month once the combined period covers at least three people. Carried-over commission is never forfeited for this reason, however long it takes. Your dashboard shows when the current month is carrying over.

## How Cloak counts people without keeping a list of them

To apply the three-person rule, Cloak has to tell people apart without keeping a record of who they are:

* Cloak never keeps a permanent record of which wallet came through which link. The referral itself lives only on the referred person's device.
* To count distinct people, Cloak stores a **pseudonymous mark** per person per month, made with a secret that is unique to that month. The mark is a one-way code: it does not contain the wallet address and cannot be turned back into it, and marks from different months cannot be matched to each other.
* When a month is paid out, its marks and its secret are deleted. A month that carries over keeps them only until it is paid out.
* Nothing about affiliates is ever written on-chain.
