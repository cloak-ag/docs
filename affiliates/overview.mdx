Cloak's affiliate program pays approved affiliates for bringing new people to Cloak. You share a personal link. For two calendar months after someone opens it, you earn a share of the percentage fee Cloak collects when that person sends, withdraws or swaps. Commissions are settled once a month, converted to USDC, and claimed to your wallet.

Nobody pays more because of it. Commissions come out of the fee Cloak already collects, so a person who arrives through your link pays exactly the same [fees](/guide/fees) as everyone else.

<Note>
Registration is closed. Every application is reviewed by hand, and the program starts with a small group of invited affiliates.
</Note>

## The rules at a glance

| Rule | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Commission | 40% of the **percentage** fee (the 0.3% part) by default. The fixed fee is never shared. |
| What earns | Private sends to an address, withdrawals, and private swaps. Shielding and shielded transfers are free, so they earn nothing. |
| Your link | `https://cloak.ag/r/<your-code>`. It never expires while you are an approved affiliate. |
| Commission window | 2 calendar months from the moment a person opens your link. |
| Who counts | New users only, attributed on their device. The first link a device opens wins, and a device is attributed at most once, ever. |
| Where | Cloak's own web app at [cloak.ag](https://cloak.ag) and the Cloak mobile apps. Activity through third-party apps, sites or SDK integrations earns nothing. |
| Self-referral | Transactions sent from your own payout wallet earn nothing. |
| Payment | USDC only, once a month. SOL and USDT fees are converted at a recorded reference price. |
| Minimum payout | None. |
| Claim window | 90 days from the day a statement opens for claiming. |
| Identity | No KYC. Your payout wallet is screened against sanctions lists. |

The details are in [How commission works](/affiliates/commission), [Payouts and claiming](/affiliates/payouts), and [Why your dashboard is aggregated](/affiliates/dashboard-privacy). The conduct rules are in the [affiliate terms](/affiliates/terms).

## How to apply

1. **Open the affiliate page.** Go to [cloak.ag/afiliados](https://cloak.ag/afiliados). In the mobile app, open **More** and tap **Affiliates**, which opens the same page in your browser.
2. **Connect your payout wallet.** The wallet you connect becomes your payout wallet: every commission you ever claim is sent to it, and only to it. Use a wallet you control and intend to keep.
3. **Choose your code.** Your code becomes your link, `https://cloak.ag/r/<your-code>`. It must be 3 to 24 characters of lowercase letters, digits and underscores. Codes that contain "cloak", reserved words such as "support", "official" or "admin", and codes that look like an existing code (for example, the same letters with `0` in place of `o`) are refused, so nobody can pass themselves off as Cloak or as another affiliate.
4. **Accept the terms and sign.** Add an optional note about where you plan to share your link, and an optional way to contact you. Then sign a message with your wallet. Signing is not a transaction: it costs nothing and moves no funds. The message your wallet shows begins with `CLOAK_AFFILIATE_APPLY_V1` and contains your code and wallet address.
5. **Wait for review.** Your application shows as **pending** until someone at Cloak reviews it by hand. Once it is approved, your link starts working and your dashboard opens.

One wallet can hold one application and one affiliate account. A code that was rejected stays reserved and cannot be reused.

### No KYC, but sanctions screening

Cloak does not ask for your name, ID documents or address. Your payout wallet is screened against sanctions lists when you apply, again before approval, whenever you change your payout wallet, and before every claim. A wallet that fails screening cannot be approved and cannot claim.

## Your account status

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Pending | Your application, or a payout wallet change, is waiting for review. |
| Approved | Your link works and you earn commission. |
| Rejected | The application was not accepted. The code stays reserved. |
| Suspended | Your link stops attributing new people and you earn nothing while suspended. Your statements are held until the suspension is lifted. See the [terms](/affiliates/terms#suspension-and-removal). |
| Removed | The account is closed for good. |

## Changing your payout wallet

Your payout wallet can only be changed with signatures from **both** the current and the new wallet. The change puts your account back into review, and your statements are held (not lost) until Cloak approves the new wallet. The new wallet is screened like any other. If you have lost access to your current wallet, contact Cloak through one of the [official channels](/guide/verified-addresses#official-links).

## Your link is the only genuine one

Genuine affiliate links always start with `https://cloak.ag/r/`. A link on any other domain is not a Cloak link, whatever it claims. When someone opens a genuine link, Cloak strips the code from the address bar and takes them to the normal app. The link carries no funds and asks for nothing: if a "referral" page asks for a seed phrase, a private key, or a deposit to unlock something, it is a scam.
