The binding terms of the Cloak Partner Program (the affiliate program described in these pages) are published at **[cloak.ag/affiliates/terms](https://cloak.ag/affiliates/terms)**. The current version is **2026-10**, approved on October 6, 2026. The program is run by Cloak Privacy Corp.

<Note>
This page is a short summary to help you find your way. It is not the terms. Where this summary and the published terms differ, the published terms apply.
</Note>

## How you accept them

You accept the terms when you apply: the application message you sign with your wallet records the terms version you accepted. That version governs your participation until you accept a newer one. Cloak notifies you in your dashboard at least 15 days before a change that reduces your commission or adds obligations takes effect, and you accept a new version by signing it in your dashboard. If you do not, you may leave the program, and statements already issued remain claimable.

## Key points

* **Eligibility.** You must be at least 18 and legally able to agree to the terms. One partner account per person or organization. You may not take part if you live in, or act for someone in, a country or region subject to comprehensive sanctions, and your wallets are screened against sanctions lists.
* **Approval.** Registration is closed. Cloak reviews every application by hand and may approve or decline any application without giving a reason.
* **Attribution.** A person becomes your referral when they open your link and then use Cloak's own web or mobile app on the same device, for 2 calendar months from their first click. First click wins, one attribution per device, new users only, and your own wallets, or wallets you control, never earn you commission. Third-party integrations built with the Cloak SDK are not part of the program.
* **Commission.** 20% of the percentage part of the fee on withdrawals and swaps made by your referrals during their window. The fixed fee, fee-free activity such as deposits and private transfers, and failed, reverted or refunded transactions earn nothing. Commission is computed and paid per token (SOL, USDC or USDT), rounded down to the token's smallest unit, with no conversion between tokens. A rate change applies only to the future and never reduces commission already earned in a closed month.
* **Statements and payment.** Each month closes on the 1st, is reconciled against on-chain fee records and is approved by two different finance approvers. A statement is issued only when at least 3 different referrals contributed; below that, commission carries over instead of being lost. You claim from the 5th, within 90 days, after which the amount stays with Cloak. Each token is paid by a private withdrawal through Cloak to your registered payout wallet, with no minimum, and Cloak pays the network costs.
* **Payout wallet.** Payments go only to your registered payout wallet, and you are responsible for keeping control of it. A change needs signatures from both the current and the new wallet and is reviewed by Cloak.
* **Promotion.** Disclose that you are a Cloak partner where the law or the platform requires it, and describe Cloak accurately. Spam, bidding on Cloak brand terms, lookalike domains or handles, forced clicks or cookie stuffing, paying people to sign up or transact without written approval, fake or self-dealing activity, and promoting Cloak for illegal activity are prohibited.
* **Privacy.** Your dashboard shows totals only, about a day late and only once at least 3 different referrals contributed. You never see who your referrals are, their wallets or their transactions.
* **Holds, suspension and forfeiture.** Cloak may hold a statement or payment during a review, and may suspend or remove an account for a breach, a sanctions match, fraud or abuse. Commission connected to a breach, fraud or abuse is forfeited. Commission earned honestly before a removal for other reasons stays claimable on the normal schedule.
* **Taxes and relationship.** You are responsible for any taxes on your commission, and you take part as an independent partner, not as an employee, agent or representative of Cloak.

The published terms also cover the data Cloak keeps about partners, your rights over it, warranties and liability. Read them in full before you apply.
