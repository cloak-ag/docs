## How you may promote Cloak

### No spam

Do not send unsolicited messages to promote your link: no mass direct messages, no unsolicited email, no comment or reply spam, no automated posting, and no posting in communities whose rules forbid promotion.

### No paid advertising on the Cloak brand

Do not buy advertising that uses Cloak's name or brand. That includes bidding on "Cloak" or any variation of it as a search keyword, running ads that look like they come from Cloak, and registering domains, social handles or app listings that use or imitate the Cloak name. Your affiliate link must always be a genuine `https://cloak.ag/r/<your-code>` link; do not disguise it or send people through lookalike pages.

### No claims about evading authorities

Do not present Cloak as a way to evade law enforcement, taxes, sanctions or any legal obligation, and do not promise anonymity from authorities. Cloak is a privacy tool with [accountable privacy](/learn/privacy-is-not-anonymity): users can prove their own history with viewing keys, and wallets are screened against sanctions lists. Describe it that way.

### Disclose that you earn a commission

Every promotion that includes your link must clearly say that you may earn a commission from it, in a way the audience sees before they click (for example "affiliate link" or "#ad" next to the link, or the equivalent disclosure your platform requires). ### Be accurate (proposed)

Describe Cloak's features, fees and limits as they are documented on this site. Do not promise returns, rewards or earnings to the people you refer, and do not claim to represent Cloak or to offer support on its behalf.

## How commission is earned

The commission rules are described in [How commission works](/affiliates/commission) and [Payouts and claiming](/affiliates/payouts). In particular:

* Commission is a fixed 20% of the percentage fee of fee-bearing transactions made in Cloak's own web and mobile apps by new users attributed to your link, for two calendar months from their click. The fixed fee is never shared.
* Transactions from your own payout wallet never earn commission.
* Creating fake referees, or manipulating attribution in any way (for example hijacking other affiliates' links), is prohibited.

## Suspension and removal

**Suspension.** Cloak may suspend an affiliate account, for example while it investigates a possible breach of these terms. While suspended:

* your link stops attributing new people, and you earn no commission;
* your unreleased and available statements are **held**: they cannot be claimed, but they are not cancelled.

If the suspension is lifted, your account is reinstated and held statements return to their previous status.

**Removal.** Cloak may close an affiliate account permanently. Removal is final. Statements that were already available for claiming remain claimable, unless Cloak also holds them.

## Forfeiture

Commission can be forfeited only in the cases below.

* **Unclaimed statements expire.** A statement that is not claimed within 90 days of opening expires and the amount stays with Cloak.
* **Breach of these terms.** Commission earned through conduct that breaks these terms may be withheld and forfeited. Which amounts, and how that is decided: *Pending legal review.* {/* OPEN-01: legal to define exactly which held amounts are forfeited on a breach, and the process for deciding it. */}

Commission is **never** forfeited because a month fell below the [three-person privacy floor](/affiliates/dashboard-privacy#the-release-floor): it carries over until the floor is met.

## Payouts

Commission is paid only in USDC, only when you claim it, only to your registered payout wallet, and only after your wallet passes sanctions screening at the time of the claim. Cloak pays each claim through a Cloak private withdrawal. There is no minimum payout. Cloak does not collect KYC information from affiliates.
