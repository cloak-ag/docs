**If your payout wallet has never held USDC or USDT**, Cloak creates its token account for that token in a separate public transaction before paying you. The commission itself is paid privately through Cloak, but that account creation is visible on chain and links Cloak to your wallet. To avoid it, receive any amount of that token in your wallet before claiming. SOL payouts are not affected.
